Collection & Payment Notice
Please read the collection schedule carefully. Your monthly earnings are stored separately and are never mixed with another month's earnings.
1

Monthly earning: Your verified ad earnings are accumulated during the current calendar month.

2

5-day collection window: The previous month's Collect button is available from the 1st through the 5th of the following month.

3

After 5 days: The Collect button closes for that month's normal collection window. The money is not deleted.

4

If you skip a month: Your uncollected amount remains stored as a separate carried-forward balance until you collect it. It does not expire simply because time has passed.

5

New month: New month's earnings belong to the new monthly bucket. They cannot be collected using the previous month's Collect button.

6

After Collect: When a collection request is successfully created, a 24-hour processing timer is shown on this page. The owner can process it earlier; otherwise the secure backend can process eligible requests after the 24-hour release time.

7

Wallet payment: After successful collection processing, the exact server-approved amount is credited to your TaskEarn wallet. The browser cannot change the amount.

8

Trusted accounting: Rewards are created only after genuine ad completion and provider verification. No fake ad completion or client-side reward amount is accepted.
