Fix your network
TaskEarn could not open Android settings automatically in this WebView. Follow the steps below.
1. Open your phone's Settings.
2. Open Network & Internet and check VPN.
3. Disable any active VPN if TaskEarn requires a direct connection.
4. Open Private DNS and use Automatic if required.
5. Disable system-wide ad blockers, DNS filtering or firewall apps.
Why are these checks required?
Some network configurations can prevent advertising and required TaskEarn services from loading. TaskEarn checks several independent signals instead of relying on one request.
Ad filtering Multiple advertising resources and browser bait checks are tested.
VPN Actual Android VPN status is used when the native APK bridge exists.
Private DNS Android native status is preferred over unreliable browser guesses.
Safe detection Unknown conditions are not falsely reported as confirmed VPN or blocker.
