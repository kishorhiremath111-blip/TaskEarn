TaskEarn Privacy

Privacy Policy

Understand how TaskEarn may collect, use and protect information associated with your account.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, TaskEarn may process account details, task submissions, reward information, withdrawal information and technical information needed to operate the service.

2. Account Information

Account information may be used for authentication, account management, task participation and reward services.

3. Payment Information

When you request a withdrawal, relevant payment-account information may be processed to verify and complete the request.

TaskEarn should never ask for your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV or OTP.

4. Task & Submission Data

Information submitted while completing tasks or projects may be processed to verify eligibility, review submissions and calculate applicable rewards.

5. How Information Is Used

  • Provide and maintain TaskEarn services.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Process task and project submissions.
  • Manage rewards and withdrawals.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Improve reliability and security.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

TaskEarn may use third-party infrastructure or service providers for authentication, databases, payments, analytics or other technical functions required to operate the service.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained as necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and available features, you may have rights relating to your personal information. Contact Support for privacy-related requests.

10. Age & Eligibility

Some TaskEarn features or payment services may have age, KYC or legal eligibility requirements. Use only features for which you are legally eligible.

11. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements or data practices change.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the TaskEarn Support page.
